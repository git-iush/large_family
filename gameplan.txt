Using OLS
Data Source: IPUMS USA
dummy variable: eligible (eligible for medicaid/welfare)
dependent variable: nchild

filters: sex
         age
         stateicp

independent: peakage (18-35 fertile ==1, 35+ ==0)
             education
             hrswrkd
             eligible_peakage
             personalincome
             familyincome/famsize
             movedin
             poverty
             hispanic
             race
             hcovany

disabilities: diffrem, diffphys, diffmob, diffcare, diffsens


model: numownchildren = b0 + b1(eligible) + b2(peakage) + b3(eligible_peakage) + controls + demographics
